About

I trained as an actor at Florida State and spent my spare time working on film-school sets. Then I spent years teaching movement, a job that is mostly about noticing small things.

In August 2026 I started weekly calls with my friend Elwyn to learn how to make good videos. I had never opened DaVinci Resolve. I learned editing, graphics and motion from zero, delivered my first paid edit in October, and my first long-form project in November.

Taste, for me, grows out of appreciation: curiosity and interest that make the invisible visible, through distinctions and felt resonance. It isn't inherited; it's cultivated through engagement. My habit is simple: notice when I like something, then make it explicit. Why does this cut land? Why does that graphic feel right?
